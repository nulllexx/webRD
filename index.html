A Minecraft SMP for everyone

Ever wanted to play multiplayer and failed to find a server that fits? The big ones wear thin after a week, and searching for an SMP mostly turns up dead worlds. BakoSMP is the other thing: a survival server small enough to feel like somewhere, run by people who are still here.

Java Edition, cracked clients included. Joining is free; the Discord is the front door.
Join our Discord Browse world downloads

Why here

six reasons

A community, not a lobby

Small enough that people know each other, active enough that the world keeps changing while you are away.

Custom plugins and features

Written for this server rather than bought off a marketplace, so they fit how people here actually play.

Events and competitions

Build contests, races and one-off chaos, announced in Discord and voted on by whoever turns up.

Staff who are actually around

Griefing and rollbacks get handled the same day, not whenever somebody next logs in.

Regular updates

Version bumps, plugin fixes and quality-of-life changes ship continuously, not once a season.

Cracked clients welcome

Java Edition only. You do not need to have bought the game to play here — you do need our Discord.

Seasons

every 2–3 months
Season 8 running world reset every 2–3 months

A new world before the old one gets boring

Every season wipes the map and brings something different with it — new mods one time, a different plugin set the next, occasionally pure vanilla. Nobody arrives six months late to a world where everything worth doing has already been done.

Getting in

three steps
  1. Join the Discord server. It is required — the whitelist and every announcement live there.
  2. Ask for whitelisting in the right channel. Someone will get to it.
  3. Point your Java client at SERVER@bakosmp.go.ro and start digging.
An account on this site is separate. You only need one for skin uploads, polls and settings — not to play.
